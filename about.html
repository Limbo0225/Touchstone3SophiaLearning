Our Story

Founded in 2015, North Star Bakery began as a small family kitchen dedicated to bringing authentic, traditional baking back to the community.

Our Mission

We believe in simple, high-quality ingredients and patient baking processes. Every loaf of sourdough is fermented for 24 hours to ensure rich flavor and digestible nutrition.
